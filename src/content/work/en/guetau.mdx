## Problem

In the 2009–2010 period, most health information online was still presented in a formal, institution-oriented style. GueTau.com took a different approach: delivering information on reproductive health, HIV, healthy lifestyles, and youth issues in a format that felt friendlier and easier for a younger generation to access.

The portal also served as a publishing outlet for articles, infographics, health campaigns, and educational material aimed at young communities — themes aligned with the youth-health and public-education focus of that period.

## Solution

The website was designed as a content portal with fairly active publishing needs. Elements built included:

- A magazine-style homepage with featured articles
- A category system for health, lifestyle, and campaign topics
- An infographics and visual-education page
- A newsletter subscription form
- Search and navigation structure to help readers find content

The look used a red-and-white color combination as the portal's visual identity, with a layout that highlighted lead articles and health-campaign material.

## Tools

- WordPress
- Custom Theme Development
- SEO On-Page
- Website Maintenance
- Content Portal

## Role in the Project

Handled the website's technical development and management, focused on user experience and search-engine visibility. The scope of work covered:

- Custom WordPress development with a design built specifically for the portal's identity
- Implementing page structure, article categories, and content archives
- On-page SEO so articles were easier for Google to index
- WordPress maintenance — system updates, plugins, and periodic upkeep
- Technical support for content publishing and editorial needs

## Outcome

- GueTau.com became one of the earliest projects in web development and SEO, laying the foundation for later work in the NGO, health, and other content-driven organization sectors.
- The website is no longer active, but the project remains part of the experience of building digital media that combined design, content management, and search-engine optimization in a single platform.
