## Problem

The website was built as Baby Jim Aditya's digital identity hub — a public figure. The goal wasn't just an online profile, but a space showcasing career milestones, work, activity documentation, and an official reference point for clients and media.

When the project started, the main requirement was a website that would:

- represent Baby Jim Aditya's identity and character,
- be easy to update without complex technical processes,
- support multiple content types such as profile, gallery, video, and activity information,
- stay relevant as an official communication channel over the long term.

## Solution

The approach positioned the website as a personal-branding asset that could keep being updated as activities and work evolved. The site was designed with a structure that's simple yet easy to grow, covering:

- Profile and career-journey page
- Photo gallery and activity documentation
- Video and work page
- Agenda / activity information
- Contact form as the official communication channel

The platform was built on **WordPress** so content management could be handled more easily without depending on a development process every time an update was needed.

## Role in the Project

Responsible for the website's development process, from page-structure design through implementation and maintenance of the WordPress-based site. The work focused on user experience, ease of content management, and a look consistent with personal-branding needs.

## Outcome

- Trusted to manage and maintain Baby Jim Aditya's official website continuously since 2012 to the present.
- The website remains an official reference point for clients and media, with a structure that's easy to update as Baby Jim Aditya's activities and work evolve.
