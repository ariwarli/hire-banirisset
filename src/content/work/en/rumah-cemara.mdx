## Problem

Rumah Cemara is a nonprofit organization working in health, social inclusion, and community empowerment, focused especially on people living with HIV (ODHIV) and people in recovery from drug use. The organization needed an official website on its own domain (rumahcemara.or.id) to serve as an information hub, program documentation, and a communication channel with the public.

When the project started, Rumah Cemara needed a website that could support several organizational functions at once:

- introducing the organization's profile and mission,
- publishing programs, activities, and news,
- serving as an easily accessible documentation archive,
- strengthening the organization's presence in search engines.

## Solution

The approach was to build a website the internal team could manage easily, so content could be updated independently. The site was designed as an organizational information portal able to accommodate both public communication and program documentation. Key features built included:

- Organization profile page
- Programs and activities page
- News and articles portal
- Documentation gallery
- Contact and organization information page
- Content structure supporting regular publishing

The implementation focused not just on the look, but also on making content easy for Rumah Cemara's team to manage.

## Role in the Project

Responsible for the entire website development process, from the technical side through implementation. The scope of work covered:

- Building the WordPress website from scratch on the organization's official domain
- Custom WordPress design tailored to the organization's visual identity
- Designing page structure, news categories, and program documentation
- On-page SEO to support content visibility on Google
- WordPress maintenance — system updates, plugins, and technical support after launch

## Outcome

- The Rumah Cemara project became one of the earliest experiences building a nonprofit organization website from scratch on WordPress.
- This experience grew into a range of website projects for NGOs, health organizations, advocacy networks, donor institutions, and other bodies.
